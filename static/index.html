PROBLEM STATEMENT

ICU capacity constraints during surge scenarios require transparent, objective, and auditable prioritization.

EXISTING APPROACHES

Legacy systems rely on point-in-time organ failure scores (SOFA, SAPS-I) or static triage guidelines.

IDENTIFIED RESEARCH GAP

CareGrid bridges physiological failure scoring and capacity management by combining organ failure, survival likelihood, and waiting duration equity.

CLINICAL SAFEGUARD

CareGrid operates strictly as clinician decision support. Final ICU admission decisions remain under clinician control.

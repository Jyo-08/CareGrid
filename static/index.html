RESEARCH & LITERATURE

Academic grounding and identified research gap

PROBLEM STATEMENT

ICU capacity constraints during surge scenarios require transparent, objective, and auditable prioritization. Legacy manual queuing is vulnerable to human fatigue and subjective variation.

EXISTING APPROACHES

Legacy systems rely on point-in-time mortality scores (SOFA, SAPS-I, NEWS2) or static paper-based triage protocols. They do not dynamically re-arbitrate candidate queues when waiting times advance or bed availability shifts.

IDENTIFIED RESEARCH GAP & CAREGRID APPROACH

CareGrid bridges the gap between physiological failure scoring and capacity management by combining SOFA-derived organ failure severity, survival likelihood, and waiting duration equity into an event-sourced deterministic arbitration engine.

CLINICAL SAFEGUARD

CareGrid operates as a clinical decision-support system. Final ICU admission and treatment decisions remain strictly under clinician control.
